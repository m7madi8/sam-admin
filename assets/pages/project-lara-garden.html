Project Overview

This garden transformation focuses on creating a balanced outdoor experience with shaded seating, layered greenery, and clear circulation paths. The layout guides visitors through intimate lounge pockets while keeping the open lawn as the central anchor.

Design Goals

  • Establish a calm, resort-like atmosphere for family gatherings.
  • Blend hardscape materials with soft planting for visual depth.
  • Introduce warm lighting for evening usability and safety.
  • Define zones for dining, conversation, and relaxation.

Client Experience

The client wanted a garden that feels inviting in every season. We designed flexible seating and a lighting plan that highlights focal points while keeping pathways bright and comfortable.

Key Details

Location Ramallah, Palestine
Project Type Residential Landscape
Area 420 m² (approx.)
Scope Concept + Full Outdoor Styling
Duration 6 Weeks
Style Modern Mediterranean

Materials & Features

Stone & Timber Decking

Natural textures in warm tones to complement the surrounding architecture.

Layered Planting

Evergreen structure with seasonal blooms for year-round vibrancy.

Outdoor Lighting Plan

Low-glare fixtures to highlight paths, seating zones, and feature greenery.

Entertaining Zones

Comfortable seating and dining areas designed for gatherings and quiet moments.
